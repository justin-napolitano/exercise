I created this repository, [exercise](https://github.com/justin-napolitano/exercise), as a straightforward way to keep track of my personal exercise goals and routines. The concept evolved after I moved to a less walkable environment. Suddenly, staying active turned into a challenge. I needed a better way to maintain my fitness amid distractions. 

## What Is This Repo Doing?

This repo isn’t just about logging exercises; it’s about building a structured plan to stay committed. Here's what you’ll find inside:

- **Daily exercise goals:** I lay out what I hope to achieve each day.
- **Detailed sections on exercise types:** Whether it's cardio, calisthenics, or balance workouts, I've made it clear.
- **Personal reflections:** I jot down what works, what doesn’t, and any adjustments I need to make.

It’s essentially a personal workout journal without the complexity of a full-blown health app.

## Why This Repo Exists

After relocating to an area without easy access to walking trails or gyms, I needed something to keep me accountable. The motivation to stay active can wane when taking walks isn’t as easy as it used to be. 

Inspiration came from wanting to track my progress without overcomplicating the process. I didn't want to create yet another fitness app that drowned in features. Instead, I wanted a clean slate to document goals and hurdles. That’s where this repo comes in.

## Key Design Decisions

I opted for a minimalistic approach. The goal was clarity, so I chose Markdown as my documentation format. It’s simple but effective. Here’s my reasoning:

- **Markdown:** It’s universally readable. Editing in Markdown is a breeze, and it doesn’t require special software. Any text editor will do.
- **Static content:** There’s no programming language involved here, just a single Markdown file, `index.md`, to keep things low-key. I appreciate the simplicity of written content when it comes to reflecting on my fitness journey.

### Project Structure

The layout of my repo is straightforward. You’ll find:

```
exercise/
└── index.md    # Main document outlining my exercise goals and routines
```

Just one file to rule them all. This keeps me focused on what matters rather than getting lost in multiple documents.

## Tech Stack

As mentioned, my tech stack is minimal:

- **Markdown**: For all the documentation
- **No frameworks**: Just raw markdown—clean and efficient.

This project is a testament to the idea that you don’t always need a complex setup to create something useful. Sometimes, simplicity is the best approach.

## Tradeoffs

Of course, keeping it simple comes with tradeoffs:

- **No automations**: I won’t be tracking my stats automatically. It’s all manual for now, which can be a drag but keeps me engaged.
- **Limited interactive features**: Right now, it lacks charts or visual analytics—just my words. It’s great for reflection, but not ideal for deeper data insight.
- **Not in web format**: For now, it’s just a document. A web version would be easier to access but would require more development work.

## Future Work / Roadmap

Looking ahead, I've got a few ideas. Here’s what I plan to tackle next:

- **Progress tracking**: I want to incorporate sections for tracking my progress in more detail. It’d be rewarding to see that growth visually over time.
- **Multimedia content**: Ever thought about showing what I’m doing? Adding images or videos to illustrate my routines would be a game changer.
- **Web format**: I’m considering converting my Markdown file into a simple website. That’ll allow for easy access and maybe even a better presentation of my journey.
- **Fitness app integration**: I’d love to explore ways to connect this with existing fitness apps to avoid the redundancy of keeping everything manual.

## Wrap Up

This repository is my personal fitness documentation tool. It’s straightforward, no-nonsense, and focused on growth. I share updates and insights about my journey on social platforms like Mastodon, Bluesky, and Twitter/X, so if you’re interested in following along, feel free to check those out.

Whether you’re into fitness or just dipping your toes in, I hope you find some value in what I’m doing here. Exercise doesn’t need to be complicated; it just needs to be consistent.
